# Tax Simulator Architecture

**Path**: `src/utils/taxEngine.ts`

## Core Purpose
Calculate Singapore Income Tax (IRAS) for the client based on their gross monthly income, projecting annual tax liabilities and potential tax savings through strategic vehicles (e.g., SRS).

## Logic & Brackets (YA 2024+)
- **First $20,000**: 0%
- **Next $10,000**: 2%
- **Next $10,000**: 3.5%
- **Next $40,000**: 7%
- **Next $40,000**: 11.5%
- **Next $40,000**: 15%
- **Next $40,000**: 18%
- **Next $40,000**: 19%
- **Next $40,000**: 19.5%
- **Next $220,000**: 20%
- **Next $500,000**: 22%
- **Next $500,000**: 23%
- **Above $1,000,000**: 24%

## Deductions (Reliefs)
- **SRS (Supplementary Retirement Scheme)**: Caps at $15,300 per year for Singapore Citizens / PRs. The engine dynamically calculates the exact tax savings generated by maxing out this relief.

## Output Structure
- `annualIncome`: Total gross income.
- `chargeableIncome`: Income after SRS and standard reliefs.
- `taxPayable`: Total final tax bill.
- `effectiveTaxRate`: Percentage of total income paid to tax.
- `srsTaxSavings`: Exact dollar amount saved by utilizing the SRS strategy.
